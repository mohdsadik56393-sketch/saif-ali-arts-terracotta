Our Story

Saif Ali Arts & Terracotta keeps a traditional craft alive for the modern home, working out of Chak Fazalpur village in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh. Every piece is shaped by hand and fired in a kiln, holding on to the natural strength and character of raw clay.

"Every piece of clay carries its own story — we simply give it shape, and make sure it reaches you with the same care it was made." — Safeek Ahmad, Founder

We make every kind of terracotta product — everyday pots and planters, decorative pieces, tableware, and handcrafted collectibles for the home. Custom orders are welcome for those who want something made to their own design.

Every order is packed and checked personally before it leaves our workshop, so you receive it exactly as it was made — with care, and without compromise on quality.
